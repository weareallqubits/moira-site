Western Astrology

The Architecture of Who You Are

What it reads

The position of the planets at the exact moment you were born. Your natal chart is a snapshot of the sky at your first breath. It shows the archetypal energies that shaped you.

What it reveals

Your core identity (Sun), your emotional needs (Moon), your mind (Mercury), your love nature (Venus), your drive (Mars), your expansion (Jupiter), your structure (Saturn), and more. Your Ascendant is the face you show the world. Your Midheaven is your public calling.

How we use it

We calculate your natal chart using the Swiss Ephemeris, the same professional grade astronomical database used by NASA and professional astrologers. We track the current positions of the planets against your natal positions (transits) to show you exactly what is being activated in your life right now, with specific dates.

Vedic Astrology

Your Karmic Blueprint

What it reads

The position of the planets using the sidereal zodiac, which accounts for the precession of the equinoxes. Vedic astrology (also called Jyotish) is the oldest continuous astrological tradition on earth, practiced in India for over 5,000 years.

What it reveals

Your karmic patterns, your life purpose (dharma), the obstacles you came here to face, and the timing of major life events through the dasha system. Vedic astrology sees the soul journey you are on and the lessons you signed up for.

How we use it

We calculate your Vedic chart using the Lahiri ayanamsa, the most widely used ayanamsa in Jyotish. We identify your ascendant (lagna), your moon sign (rashi), your nakshatra (lunar mansion), and your current dasha period so you know which karmic chapter you are in right now.

What that looks like in real life“You are in a Saturn dasha, which is the universe’s way of saying: slow down and get serious about what actually matters. Saturn rewards discipline and strips away everything superficial. This is not a punishment. This is a pruning. Your Vedic chart has Saturn in your 10th house of career, which means this period is about stepping into authority in your work life. Not next year. Now. Start the thing you have been putting off.”

Sabian Symbols

The Images of Your Soul

What it reads

Every single degree of the zodiac, all 360, has a specific symbolic image associated with it. These images were channeled by clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler in 1925 and have been used by astrologers for a century.

What it reveals

A specific visual image and interpretation for the exact degree of each of your planets. Your Sun at 6 degrees Pisces has a specific Sabian Symbol. So does your Moon, your Ascendant, every placement. Each symbol is a visual metaphor that describes a specific life theme.

How we use it

We map every planetary position in your chart to its exact Sabian Symbol degree. We provide the symbol image and interpretation for your Sun, Moon, Ascendant, and key planets. This adds a visual, poetic layer to the other systems. Where astrology says ’your Sun is in Pisces,’ the Sabian Symbol says ’your Sun is in the degree of the cross illuminated by a shaft of light on rocks surrounded by sea.’ That image tells a story that the sign name alone cannot.

What that looks like in real life“Your Sun’s Sabian Symbol is ’Illuminated by a shaft of light, a large cross lies on rocks surrounded by sea and mist.’ This image speaks to your life theme: you are someone who finds meaning in liminal spaces, the places where the sea meets the shore, where the known meets the unknown. Your gift is bringing the light of understanding to places that feel desolate. Do not be afraid of the mist. It is where you do your best work.”
